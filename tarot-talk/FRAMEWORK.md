# The Tarot Talk framework

Status: v2, revised after an independent review (reviews/2026-11-20-framework-review.md). The v2 changes have not been re-checked.

How Tarot Talk plans a series and writes an episode. `SKILL.md` holds the working rules: order of work, the stop / follow / choose gates, how it looks, honesty rules, and the rule that nobody approves their own work. If the two files differ, `SKILL.md` wins.

## 1. What we are building

A series, not single reels: 10 to 20 episodes of 30-60 seconds, one look, one story, one product at the end.
The path: reel -> comment one keyword (a word, not a digit) -> auto-DM -> paid report, healing or guidance.
The reel gives the general reading, complete in itself. The paid product gives the personal one, and it must exist or have a delivery date before the series is made.

## 2. Start from real people

Before any script, fill `audience.md` from real material: comments, DMs, questions asked before buying, what past buyers said. Keep their own words.
Do not let an AI guess the audience from the niche name; that gives the picture everyone else has.
If there is little material yet, ask the reader for the ten questions she hears most, and add to the file after every series.

## 3. A series idea must have

- A self-check: the viewer finds themselves at once (birth date, month, name, a number they keep seeing).
- A belief to break: something the viewer already assumes, turned over. ("Same mulank, different year.")
- A counted set that truly gives 10-20 episodes by the method's own arithmetic.
- Every episode usable by every viewer, and the bible says how. An opener and a finale that work for all; a finale with real content, not a tease.
- A date worked back from: festival, year end, launch.
- For a repeat series: a true reason last time's buyer needs it again.
- Not the same shape as the series before it (never two "number 1-9" series back to back).

## 4. Every episode follows TAROT

Rough timing for a 45-second reel.

- **T — Thehro (0-3 s).** Stop the scroll and open one question. Three hooks planned together: what moves on screen, the first spoken line, and a short on-screen text that adds to the spoken line instead of repeating it. Must work with the sound off.
- **A — Apni baat (3-10 s).** "This is about me." The self-check on screen, with a small lookup card, so the viewer places themselves.
- **R — Reveal (10-38 s).** One idea only. One usable thing to keep: the theme, one do, one avoid. The question opened in Thehro is answered here, near the end. Hold back quantity (the other months, the personal reading), never the punchline. This is also where the reason to send it to someone lives.
- **O — On to the next (38-41 s).** Name the next episode in one line.
- **T — Take the step (41-45 s).** One keyword, shown and spoken, with the reason: what comes in the DM and what the report gives, in one plain sentence. Do not say "report mein" inside the episode before this.

How TAROT meets the gates: Thehro and Apni baat are the stop gate; Reveal and On to the next are the follow gate; Take the step is the choose gate.

Search words: the words people would type (mulank, personal year 2027, the series name) appear in the spoken line, the on-screen text, the caption and the cover image. Pin the keyword comment.

Worked example, 2027 driver series, episode 1:
- Thehro: two friends in one coach, both wearing a 5. The coach splits onto two tracks. Text: "Mulank same. Saal alag?"
- Apni baat: "Aapka mulank + janm ka mahina + 2" on a lookup card.
- Reveal: the engine cab; a saarthi steps out of the steam. "Hum ise aapke saal ka driver kehte hain." One friend's driver is 1, the other's is 7, and what that changes.
- On to the next: "Kal: ek mulank, teen mahine, teen alag saal."
- Take the step: "Comment DRIVER. DM mein aapka driver number aur 2027 report ki jaankari."

## 5. Measure, then fix the weakest link

Numbers to log are listed in `SKILL.md`, plus the retention graph. Read from the top of the path down, and compare each reel with the series median, not on its own.

- Few non-followers reached: the reel is not being sent or is not original enough; check Reveal for a reason to send.
- Low 3-second hold: fix Thehro.
- The graph drops in the middle: fix Apni baat and Reveal (too slow, too many ideas).
- Watched to the end but few sends: Reveal gave nothing worth passing on.
- Few follows: On to the next is weak, or episodes do not look like one series.
- Good completion but few keyword comments: fix Take the step. If completion is poor, fix the earlier parts first; nobody saw the keyword.
- Comments but few DMs opened: check the automation and the DM's first line.
- DMs opened but few sales: the offer, the price or the payment page.
