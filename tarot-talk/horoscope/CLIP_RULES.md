# Clip and picture selection rules (Tarot Talk By Sakshi)

Every clip is chosen for its exact line, then checked by an independent reviewer before any render. A clip that fails one rule is replaced.

1. Exact match. The clip shows the specific situation in the line, not just the life area. "Talk about money at home" = a family talking money or looking at bills/cash at home, not a dinner table. "White sweets" = white mithai (rasgulla, kaju barfi, kheer, peda), not orange or red sweets. Colours, objects and actions named in the line must be visible.
2. Indian for India. People are Indian and the setting looks Indian (homes, offices, clothes, festivals). Global videos use people who fit the target country.
3. Aspirational, never negative stereotypes. Middle and upper-middle class, clean, well lit, warm. Travel = flights, airports, hotels, beaches, hills, road trips; never crowded trains or stations, poverty, dirt or chaos.
4. Mood match. good = bright, smiling, confident; care = thoughtful, calm; stop = tense but not scary; sad hook = gentle and relatable; fun = playful; serious = firm; encouraging = uplifting.
5. Brand fit. Warm, feminine, spiritual-modern. The hook shows mystic or festive warmth (diya, candles, tarot, puja, festival lights). Women are the main subject where possible.
6. Quality. Vertical 9:16, HD or better, sharp, steady, subject centred and visible inside a 900x960 crop, no watermark, no on-screen text, no visible brand logos, no famous faces.
7. Licence. Free for commercial use (Pexels, Pixabay). Never film, TV, Bollywood or music-video footage, other creators' reels, or Google Images results: they get reels muted, removed or struck.
8. Variety. No clip twice in one video; rotate clips across weeks so videos do not look the same.

## Gate (mandatory)

- Build a review sheet: for each slot, 3 frames (start, middle, end) plus the exact line and its mood.
- An independent reviewer that did not choose the clips checks the sheet against rules 1 to 6 and 8 and answers PASS or FAIL per slot with a one-line reason.
- Replace every FAIL, then re-review only the replaced slots. Render only when every slot passes.
- The owner (or Sakshi) gets the passed sheet when the library or a slot type is new.
