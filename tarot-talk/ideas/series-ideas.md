# Series idea list — v3

Status: revised after independent reviews 1 and 2 (see reviews/). The v3 changes have not been re-checked by a reviewer yet. Owner has not picked.
Funnel for all: reel -> one comment keyword (a word, not a digit) -> auto-DM -> paid PDF / reading.
Wording rule for all: "pattern" and "tendency", never "will happen". No promise on money, health, or a person coming back.

Open decision for the owner: reviewer 2 says the 2027 personal-year series (idea 10) is the real money series and should be the priority, with idea 1 only as a short lead-in or dropped, because idea 1's cheap PDF may eat into the 2027 report. The owner said 2027 comes later, so it stays "held" here until the owner decides.

## Make first (order mixes number series with others)

### 1. 2026 Jaane se Pehle
Before this year closes, your mulank has one thing to let go and one thing to finish.
Nine numbers, nine goodbyes to 2026.
- Episodes: 10 (mulank 1-9 + finale: one closing ritual everyone can do on 31 Dec)
- Connection: the same self-check that already sold; year-end mood
- Sells: year-closing PDF at a low price; warms buyers for the 2027 report
- Timing: has to start about 1 December, which is about 12 days away with a new film method. Tight. Decide quickly or skip.

### 2. Aapka Janm Tarot Card
Your birth date adds up to a pair of tarot cards that stay with you for life. Find yours.
- Episodes: 10 (the nine birth-card groups + finale: how to work with your cards)
- Connection: tarot itself at the centre; easy self-check; different feel from the mulank series
- Sells: personal tarot report (PDF) or a card reading

### 3. Mulank Jodi
Your number and theirs: where you two click, where you rub, and the one habit that helps.
Check your date, then check theirs.
- Episodes: 10 (mulank 1-9, each = best match, hardest match, one thing that helps; finale = how to read any two numbers together)
- Connection: viewer checks self, then partner. Wedding season runs to February.
- Sells: couple compatibility report (PDF)

## Next in line

### 4. Yeh Number Baar Baar Kyun Dikhta Hai?
111 on the clock, 222 on a bill, 555 on a number plate. Twelve repeating numbers and what each is nudging you to notice.
- Episodes: 12 (000, 111 ... 999, 1111, 1212)
- Connection: everyone has noticed one
- Sells: needs a firm product first — proposal: "Aapka Number, Aapka Sandesh" PDF reading the repeating number against the buyer's date of birth

### 5. Aapki Janm Tareekh Mein Kaunse Number Gayab Hain?
Write your birth date in a 3x3 grid. Some boxes stay empty — each empty box is something you keep working for.
- Episodes: 10 (missing 1-9, one per episode + finale: several are missing, which one to read first)
- Connection: viewer fills the Lo Shu grid in their head; the grid is made for animation
- Sells: Lo Shu grid report (PDF)

### 6. Mobile Number ka Sach
You picked your phone number without thinking. Add its digits and see what pattern it carries.
- Episodes: 10 (totals 1-9 + finale: how to read a number before you take it)
- Connection: viewer adds up their own number on the spot; spreads in family groups
- Sells: mobile number report (PDF). The reel reads only the total and says so; the report reads digit pairs.

### 7. Aap Kis Mahine Mein Paida Hue?
The month you arrived in left a mark: your gift, your soft spot, and what you came to learn.
- Episodes: 12
- Connection: self-recognition, shared around birthdays
- Sells: janm-maas report (PDF)

### 8. Naam ka Pehla Akshar
The first letter of your name is the first sound people give you all your life. What does yours draw in?
- Episodes: 20, English spelling: 16 single letters (A S R K M P N D V J B G H T L C) + 4 grouped (E-I, O-U, F-Q-X, W-Y-Z). Order shown in episode 1.
- Connection: instant self-check, sent to friends
- Sells: full-name numerology report (PDF). The reel says the full name tells more.

## Held for a date

### 9. Devi ke 9 Roop — kis shakti ko pukaarein
Nine nights, nine forms, nine strengths. Which one do you need to call on this season?
- Episodes: 10 (intro on 6 April 2027 + one form each night of Chaitra Navratri, 7-15 April 2027; recheck the dates for an Indian city before scheduling)
- Connection: devotion and the mythology-motion trend. Told as strength to call on; no weakness, no home-made number mapping stated as scripture.
- Sells: guided nine-day practice (PDF/audio). Needs a sensitivity review before any script.

### 10. 2027: Aapka Personal Year
Your birth day + birth month + 2027 gives one number. That number is the mood of your whole year.
- Episodes: 10 (personal year 1-9 + finale: how to find your number for each month). The reel says it uses the calendar-year method.
- Connection: new number for everyone, including last year's buyers
- Sells: the 2027 yearly report

## Parked or dropped (with reason)
- Soulmate, Karmic ya Sirf Aadat?: strongest hook, but no self-check, and a "karmic" label can keep someone in a harmful relationship. Parked until it has a safety line and a structure a reviewer accepts.
- Pick a Card: a weekly slot, not a series. Can run alongside any series.
- Kaunsa Chakra Band Hai?: only 7 real episodes, and healing claims are risky.
- Lakshmi money stories: no self-check for the viewer; stories need real sources.
- Past life by birth month: the mapping was invented. Dropped fully.
