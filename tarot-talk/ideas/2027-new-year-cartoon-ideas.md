# 2027 New Year cartoon series ideas — DRAFT v1 (not reviewed)

Goal: viewer gets curious about their own 2027 and books the 2027 yearly report (comment keyword -> auto-DM).
Common device: every episode shows part of the answer and visibly holds one part back for the report.
Self-check: mulank (birth date 1-9), as in the 2026 series that sold. Each series = 9 episodes + opener/finale = 10-12.

## A. Nau Number, Ek Mohalla — "2027 ka mehmaan"
Nine numbers live in one lane as nine cartoon people: bossy 1, soft-hearted 2, chatterbox 3, strict uncle 8 and so on.
On New Year's night a stranger called 2027 knocks on each door with a tied potli. It opens only halfway.
- Each episode: one door. The potli shows one gift and one caution for that number; the third thing stays wrapped. "Teesri cheez report mein."
- Why it connects: people see themselves in the character ("main toh pakka 5 hoon"), tag family by number. The cast is reusable all year.

## B. 2027 ki Chitthi
A cartoon postman cycles in with a letter for you — written by you, on 31 December 2027.
"Yaad hai March mein kya hua tha?" ... and the ink is smudged right there.
- Each episode: one mulank's letter from their future self. Two lines readable, the key line smudged.
- Why it connects: a letter from yourself feels personal and hopeful, not like a prediction lecture.

## C. Aapka 2027: Trailer
Every number's 2027 is a different film: comeback story, love story, suspense, family drama.
Watch the trailer. The interval twist is cut out.
- Each episode: a 30-second cartoon movie trailer for one mulank, with poster, genre, "coming this year". Full picture = report.
- Why it connects: funny and shareable ("Mulank 7 ka 2027 suspense thriller hai"); Bollywood language everyone knows.

## D. 2027 Express
One train, twelve coaches, one for each month. Your number has a window seat.
Some stations bring sweets, one is a long tunnel. Which month is your tunnel?
- Each episode: one mulank's character rides; best station shown, one more shown, the tunnel month stays dark. Report = the full ticket, all 12 stations.
- Why it connects: month-wise curiosity is exactly what a yearly report sells; railway visuals feel Indian and warm.

## E. 2026 ne Chaabi Di
Tired old 2026 hands the keys to little 2027. Before leaving, it says sorry to each number for what it put them through.
Then 2027 leans in and whispers what it is bringing — and the sound cuts.
- Each episode: one mulank. First half names the hard thing of 2026 (viewer feels seen), second half the muted whisper.
- Why it connects: recognition of last year's pain first, then hope. Strongest emotional pull.

## F. Tarot ke Patte Lad Pade
Inside the reader's deck the cards are alive and fighting over who gets to be your card for 2027.
One card wins for each number. A second card stays face down.
- Each episode: a cartoon version of the reader shuffles; cards as characters (the clumsy Fool, the proud Emperor) argue; winner revealed. Face-down card = report.
- Why it connects: puts tarot and the reader herself on screen as a character, builds her brand, not just the report.
