# 2027 concept: "Aapka Saal Kaun Chala Raha Hai?" — DRAFT v1 (not reviewed)

## The owner's idea, in his words
"Ek mulank but different different year for different people! So it's not just mulank. Then what's hiding. Who is driving your year actually. Who is this driver we forgot to see (some shadow figure)?"

## How the author has shaped it (open to challenge)
- You are the passenger (your mulank). Every year someone else sits in the driver's seat. Nobody looks at the driver.
- The driver is a shadow figure at first: a dark outline in the engine cab of the 2027 train.
- In numerology terms the driver is the personal year number: birth day + birth month + 2027, reduced to 1-9. So there are nine possible drivers, each a cartoon character with a driving style (one races, one takes the long scenic route, one stops at every station, one clears the old track before moving).
- The same mulank with a different driver gets a different year. That is the hook and also the honest reason a 2026 buyer needs a 2027 report.

## Episode plan (12)
1. Two friends, same mulank, same compartment in 2026. In 2027 their coaches split onto different tracks. Why? Camera moves to the engine: a shadow at the controls. "Aapka saal kaun chala raha hai?"
2. How to find your driver: the 8-second sum on screen. Driver still a shadow; you now know your number, not the face.
3-11. One driver per episode. Opens on the silhouette, then the reveal. Shows the driving style and one full, useful month of the route. The other months are the report.
12. Finale: passenger + driver together. Same driver treats a mulank 1 and a mulank 7 differently. Nine drivers x nine passengers = your own route. That combination is what the report reads.

## What is held back for the report
- All 12 months of the route (each episode gives one).
- The driver x mulank combination for the buyer.
Not held back: who the driver is. The reveal is given in the reel.

## Open questions for the reviewer
- Is "shadow driver" a real curiosity hook or just a costume on personal year number?
- Should the driver's identity be revealed free (as planned) or is that giving away the product?
- Does episode 2 (teaching the sum before any reveal) kill the suspense or build it?
- Does a shadow figure driving your life read as fear (someone else controls you)? How to keep it warm?
- Is the claim honest: does personal year actually work as "driver" and mulank as "passenger" in the way numerologists use them?
- Better title options in Hinglish.
