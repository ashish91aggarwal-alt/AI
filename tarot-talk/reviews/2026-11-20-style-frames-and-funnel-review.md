# Independent review — style frames v1 and the Instagram funnel post (20 Nov 2026)

Reviewer: separate fresh agent with web search and image viewing.

## Style frames (series/2027-driver/style-frames/)
Verdict: none is pilot-ready. Develop C (gilded) using A's cleaner drawing. Rank: C, A, D, B.

Shared problems: it is a poster, not a shot (six ideas in one frame); the hidden driver is thumbnail size and smoke covers the cab; "two different drivers" is not drawn, only two small signboards; coaches float, not coupled, fork is a small X; "Driver 5" beside "Mulank 5" confuses (use a month whose driver is not 5, e.g. September gives 7); faces are generic heads with no bodies, not Indian-coded, nothing to joint; small labels too small for a phone; bottom caption sits under Instagram's own caption and buttons.

Per frame: A cleanest and easiest to rig but looks like a kids' explainer, weak stop in this feed. B worst: muddy, see-through shapes, stray lines. C strongest stop: festive, warm; faults are a heavy border, star clutter behind the headline, grey beam, pink cab, weak gold caption on green. D prettiest texture but washed out, faces smudged; p5.brush strokes are random per frame so it will flicker and render slowly; least practical for jointed animation.

Fixes before a pilot: one idea per shot, opening on the cab with the window filling a third of the frame; safe areas (top 250, bottom 400, right 150 px); no text under ~48 px, two text items at most; coaches coupled on rails with a big fork ahead; two visibly different drivers; full-body Indian-dressed jointed characters; thinner border, fewer stars, warm beam, smoke clear of the cab; judge at phone size.

## Funnel post (references/2026-11-20-instagram-funnel-post.jpg)
Verdict: useful as a list of topics; the percentages are decoration. No source found; the same 40/30/20/10 split three times suggests invented numbers.
Instagram's own guidance as reported (Social Media Today, Buffer; reviewer read summaries): top signals are watch time, likes and sends; check average watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach; sends weigh more for non-followers; originals get more distribution; trial reels go to non-followers; about half of video is watched without sound.
Missing from the post: sends, watch-through, sound-off viewing, saves, originality, trial reels, series continuity, the comment-to-DM step.
Proposed gates and post-publish numbers: taken into SKILL.md.
