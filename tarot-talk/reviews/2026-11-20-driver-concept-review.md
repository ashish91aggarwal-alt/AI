# Independent review — 2027 "driver" concept v1 (20 Nov 2026)

Reviewer: separate fresh agent, with a quick web search (one source read: bonniesorsby.com).

Verdict: strong idea, worth making; fix the one-in-nine problem and the fear framing first.

Owner's raw idea
- Strength: "same mulank, different year" breaks a belief the audience holds and gives 2026 buyers a reason to buy again.
- Weak: implies last year's report was incomplete; frame as "level 2".
- Weak: the mystery is thin; the driver is the personal year number, free on any calculator, so suspense alone cannot carry the sale.

Shaping
- Lead with the two friends splitting; the shadow driver is a good costume, not the hook itself.
- Reveal the driver free. Hold back month-by-month route, dates, driver x mulank reading, remedies. Free part per episode: year's theme + one do + one avoid.
- Do not spend a whole episode on the sum; put it at the end of episode 1 and repeat a 3-second lookup card every episode.
- "Shadow" + "you are the passenger" reads as someone else controlling you. Make the figure a saarthi hidden by steam or backlight; the passenger chooses the stations.
- Honest as a metaphor only: say "hum ise driver kehte hain". Source describes fixed number as direction, personal year as timing, nine-year cycle.

Convention: personal year = birth day + birth month + 2027 reduced to 1-9, Jan-Dec 2027; 2027 -> 11 -> 2; no master numbers. So driver = mulank + birth month + 2, reduced. Unchecked by reviewer: some run it birthday to birthday.

One-in-nine problem: still there in episodes 3-11. Fix: episodes by theme (paisa, rishte, career, ghar, sehat) each showing all nine drivers; end every episode with a mulank x birth-month lookup grid.

Risks: no guaranteed outcomes; no "bad driver"; nothing saying the 2026 report was wrong.

Strongest version (10): ep1 friends split + sum + grid; ep2 one mulank, three birth months, three years; ep3-8 one theme each, all nine drivers; ep9 same driver with mulank 1 vs 7; ep10 report pitch.

Titles: "Mulank Same, Saal Alag?", "2027 Ka Driver Kaun?", "Aapka 2027 Kaun Chala Raha Hai?"
