Key rules every session must know:
- Brand Account channel per brand; phone-verify on day one (needed for videos > 15 min and custom thumbnails).
- Name and handle can change only twice in 14 days. Our names: Page & Lantern (@PageAndLantern, Chaldean 42 → 6; always write "&") and Prayer Pastures (@PrayerPastures, 51 → 6). Both passed the name gate (reviews/brand/).
- Country of residence = India (decides Partner Program eligibility). Audience = not made for kids.
- AI use disclosure: YES on every Page & Lantern video (AI music is the main focus); worship = No only when the music is our own rendered arrangements and the scene is not photoreal. When unsure, Yes.
- Title ≤ 100 chars (key words first), description ≤ 5,000 chars (first lines matter), exactly 3 hashtags, 5–8 tags, chapters start at 00:00 (≥3, each ≥10 s), thumbnail 3840×2160 16:9 JPG/PNG.
- Banner 2560×1440 (text inside 1235×338), profile 800×800, watermark 150×150.
- Partner Program: 1,000 subs + 4,000 public watch hours / 12 months, 2-Step Verification, advanced features, AdSense; ad-campaign views don't count.
- Monetization review looks at theme, top/newest videos, metadata and About; inauthentic (mass-produced, minimal variation) and reused content are not monetizable.
