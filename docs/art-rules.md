# Art rules for thumbnails and plates (17 Nov 2026, from the owner's feedback + side-by-side with the leaders)

People click on the picture before they hear anything. The picture must win the click on its own.
Compared with the leaders (cozy cabin 52K/day, living room 40K/day, book café, castle library, Moria, white stag),
our first ChatGPT inns lost because they were evenly lit, ornate everywhere, illustration-style, seen from far away,
with a small window and a small subject.

1. **Photoreal and cinematic**, like a photo on a full-frame camera: shallow depth of field, soft bokeh, real
   textures. Not an illustration or a painting.
2. **Night, mostly dark**, lit only by warm sources (fire, tree lights, candles, lanterns). Strong contrast,
   deep shadows. Warm amber inside against deep cool blue outside.
3. **You are in the room**: camera at seated eye level, soft-focus foreground (blanket, mug, armrest).
4. **A big window to a magical view** taking 40–60% of the frame: a snowy village, castle, lake or forest with
   glowing windows, lanterns and falling snow. That is the "escape".
5. **One clear subject**, large and close (the sleeping animal on the blanket, the stag, the tree).
6. **Uncluttered**: fantasy detail in a few places only (window frame, beams), not on every surface.
7. **Christmas season**: one big tree with many tiny warm lights, by the window.
8. No text on the thumbnail unless a side-by-side test proves it wins.

# Sound rules (owner)
- The picture brings the click; the **first melody and instrument** decide whether people stay.
- Audiences come for an instrument: harp, piano, jazz, or a soft humming choir ("aah" pad). Each video promises one
  lead instrument in its title and delivers it in the first 30 seconds with the strongest melody of the set.
- Measured on the leaders: very soft and dark (median −22 to −27 dB in-page, most energy below 500 Hz), slow.
- Plan: Page & Lantern videos rotate harp-led and soft-humming-choir-led sets; piano-led for the cozy-room pocket;
  Prayer Pastures piano-led with choir "aah" under it. All made locally.
