### New problems found in Revision 2
A. **Fox breathing is too slow to be natural (owner rule 1).** The plan takes "one clean breath" from a 5 s I2V clip and stretches it to 9.0 s. A sleeping fox breathes roughly 15–25 times a minute (one breath every 2.5–4 s). One breath per 9 s is about 7 a minute, and a 3–4× slow-down of I2V frames gives interpolation smear on the fur. My round-1 note "fox breathing 9 or 10 s" was meant as the clip/cycle length and was ambiguous; I correct it here: the 9 s cycle must contain 3 natural breaths (3 s each), made with first-frame = last-frame conditioning (docs/pipeline.md), retimed by no more than ±15%.
B. Real CC0 fire footage in an oil-painting-style plate is a likely style clash (an AI/compositing tell at G1/G2), and a real fire clip is not periodic at 90 s. Prefer I2V of the hearth from the plate itself; if footage is kept, state the treatment (painterly filter, grade to plate) and the loop method (≥ 90 s clip, crossfaded seam).
C. Carol render path unstated (see fix 5).
D. Unchanged from round 1 and still a G1 risk: the "open leather book" will carry fake text at 4K. Close it or angle it away. G1 note "four legs tucked" will push the model to show all four legs; a curled sleeping fox shows at most the front paws. Say "legs tucked under the body, one tail wrapped over the nose".
E. Date realism: G1–G4 in 18–22 Nov (5 days) with reviews and any reshoots is tight but possible because the visual loop is short and the render is a stream copy.
