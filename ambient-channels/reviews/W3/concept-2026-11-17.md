### New problems found in Revision 2
A. **Sheep breathing is too slow to be natural (owner rule 1).** "One clean breath retimed to 8.0 s" = 7.5 breaths a minute. A resting adult sheep breathes about 12–20 times a minute (one breath every 3–5 s). Stretching one I2V breath to 8 s also smears the wool. Use 2 natural breaths per 8 s cycle (4 s each, 15 a minute), seamless by first = last frame conditioning, retime ≤ ±15%.
B. **Light source contradiction.** The sheep element is lit "cool blue from above by moonlight", but the plate has no moon and a "sky full of stars" (a bright moon washes most stars out). Use "cool starlight / night-sky light" in the sheep prompt, or add a thin crescent moon low in the plate and accept fewer stars. Pick one.
C. Thumbnail "sleeping sheep in silhouette by the fire": a silhouette needs the fire behind the sheep, but the sheep is lit from the left as a lit shape. Either describe it as a firelit sheep or move the fire behind it in the thumbnail crop. (G5 note.)
D. Fire: real CC0 campfire footage inside a classical-oil-painting plate is a likely style clash, and a real clip does not loop at 120 s by itself. Prefer I2V of the campfire from the plate; if footage is kept, state the painterly treatment and the loop method.
E. Date accuracy for the description credits: the VENI EMMANUEL tune appeared with Helmore's name in *Hymnal Noted* (1856); 1851 is the date of Neale's translation (hymnologyarchive.com, "The Great Antiphons"). Credit it as "15th-c. chant, adapted by Thomas Helmore, 1856" in W3.md and CHANNEL.md. PD status is unaffected. Adam 1847, Gruber 1818 and Redner 1868 are correct.
F. **Channel dependency is on the wrong date.** The plan ties the Prayer Pastures channel (pending YouTube's selfie approval) to 27 Nov, but the 3 h Private upload on 24 Nov already needs the channel to exist and be verified for > 15 min uploads. Move it to ≤ 23 Nov and say what happens if approval is late (the date slides to approval + 72 h, latest 2 Dec).
G. Small G2 notes: real star twinkle is fast and tiny; 20–40 s pulses can read as blinking lights, so keep the amplitude very low. Grass sway only "near the fire" will look odd next to still grass; extend the mask to all foreground grass, with smoke drifting the same way as the sway. Ambience at −20 / −24 dB is quieter than docs/pipeline.md's 10–14 dB under music; fine for worship, but state the values relative to the music and log the exception.
Checked and fine: olive trees, low stone terraces and a hilltop village fit the Bethlehem / Beit Sahour hills; green grass and no crickets fit a Judean winter; an unattended fire implies shepherds just out of frame (a staff and cloak by the fire would make that clear without faces; optional). A plain "white sheep" prompt may give a modern European breed; "fat-tailed Middle Eastern sheep, cream wool, brown head" is more believable (optional). One bright star over Bethlehem mixes Matthew 2 with Luke 2, but this is standard Christmas iconography and the audience expects it. Pacing W3 27 Nov / W1 29 Nov is within ≤ 1 per 2 days.

### Is the per-item timeout really hard? (read the code)
The ledger logic is hard. Before an item starts, `allowed_min()` takes the smaller of (session cap − 3 min − minutes used) and (card money left). The item is skipped if its scaled estimate does not fit, and it is passed that allowance as its timeout. In every run I made, nothing passed a limit: clips 1× to 4× slow, first clip 9×/12×/20× slow, a W3 limit of $0.60, F1 with only $0.30–1.69 left. Highest total: $2.27 against the $2.40 cap.
It is not yet hard on the real pod, for two reasons:
- **The kill is the generator's job, and the generator is not written yet.** `run()` does `minutes = min(minutes, allow)`. If the generator overruns, the ledger clamps the number and hides the overrun instead of catching it. I checked: a generator that ignores the timeout at 4× slow still shows $2.15–2.18 in the ledger. The runner should time each call itself (wall clock) and charge the real time.
- **Only item minutes are charged.** Pod boot before the runner starts, `check_clip` time, the BiRefNet matte, pushes, and (if each item is a fresh subprocess) reloading Wan's two 14B experts for every clip are not counted. So the ledger undercounts real pod time. The real backstop is `MAX_MIN` = 53 min in start.sh, and start.sh for session 2 does not exist yet.
Money exposure is still bounded once start.sh exists: each session ≤ $2.40, and even if W3 paid half of both full sessions it would be $0.40 + $1.20 + $1.20 = $2.80 ≤ $3. So the budget is safe by design. What is not yet protected is waste with no output (next point).
