## Promise
"Step into a quiet fantasy place for a few hours." Each video = one crafted place
(elven inn, wizard library, Celtic valley…) with living details, soft medieval/Celtic
music and real ambience. For reading, study, sleep, tabletop games and writing.

## Sound palette
Celtic harp, lute, nylon guitar, low whistle, wooden flute, fiddle (slow airs), cello,
soft strings, hammered dulcimer, gentle choir pads. No drums except a very soft bodhrán
or frame drum in daytime scenes. Keys: D dorian, A minor, G major, E aeolian. 56–76 BPM.
Public-domain carols keep their printed mode (melody notes are never changed); their tonic comes from
the palette (e.g. Wexford Carol in G mixolydian). Logged in docs/decisions.md.

## Visual rules
- One place per video, never reused. Rotate seasons and times of day.
- At least one living element with natural joint motion + 3 ambient layers.
- Warm grading for night/indoor; soft cool mist for dawn/outdoor.

## Cadence
Pilots: 3 videos over ~8 days. After G7 decision: 2 per week, never 2 on one day.
