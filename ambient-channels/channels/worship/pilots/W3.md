## Motion — loop 120 s; every period divides 120 s
**Working rate for all I2V layers: 16 fps** (Wan 2.x native); loop arithmetic is in 16 fps frames, and the finished 120 s loop is frame-interpolated to **30 fps** (RIFE, open, arbitrary timestep; 120 s × 30 = 3,600 frames) and seam-checked again at G2.
**Every Wan prompt includes "static locked-off camera, no camera movement".** Drift check per clip: outside the motion mask (sheep flank, or flames) the outline may move ≤ 1 px across the clip (edge-map difference). A failing clip is discarded, never stabilised by moving the picture.
| Layer | How | Period |
|---|---|---|
| Foreground sheep breathing (flank rise/fall) | Wan 2.x I2V on the sheep composite crop. **5 clips of 129 frames (8.06 s)**, the first of which is the automatic length test (fallback 113 or 97 frames; see production/gpu/session2/PLAN.md). A resting sheep breathes ≈ 15/min (4 s per breath); a native breath may be 3.48–4.71 s (the ±15% retime window), and 8.06 − 4.71 − 0.25 = 3.10 s, so a clip whose first rest point falls in its first 3.1 s holds a complete breath plus tail. At 4 s per breath a rest point always falls within the first ≈ 4 s, so each clip gives a usable breath with probability ≈ 0.95 at 4.0 s (0.66 at the slowest 4.71 s breath); with 5 clips P(≥ 2 usable) ≥ 0.95. Fallback lengths and their yields (113 frames 0.45–0.70, 97 frames 0.23–0.45, each with 2 extra clips) are in PLAN.md; the floor that needs no new session is a one-breath ring (4.0 s × 30 = 120 s), judged at G2. We need 2. The yield check is **an automatic script on the pod** (`check_clip.py`: flank-mask optical-flow signal, rest points, breath lengths); if fewer than 2 usable breaths are found it queues up to 2 more clips itself, within the cap. No person or review is in that loop, so the idle kill cannot fire while waiting. Each **segment = one breath retimed to 4.0 s + a 0.25 s rest tail = 4.25 s (68 frames)**. Join them in a ring A→B→A with **4-frame (0.25 s) crossfades** at the end-of-exhale rest points: 2 × 4.25 − 2 × 0.25 = **8.0 s exactly** (128 frames), ×15 = 120 s. Seam test: frame difference at each join below the clip's own frame-to-frame noise | 8 s |
| Campfire flames | Wan I2V on the painted campfire crop of our own plate (style stays painted). 3 different **5.5 s** clips (89 frames trimmed to 88) in a ring with 0.5 s (8-frame) crossfades = **15.0 s** pattern, ×8 = 120 s. **Only the flames are composited, through a soft feathered mask; the stones and grass stay the static plate.** The glow on the nearby grass and stones is a light-map driven by the flame layer's luminance | 120 s |
| Star twinkle (a few stars only) | local, out of phase, **very low amplitude (≤ 8% brightness change)** so it reads as air shimmer, not blinking lights | 20/30/40 s |
| Village window lamps | local light-map, very slow | 60 s |
| Grass sway | mesh warp over a mask of **all foreground grass** (not only near the fire), amplitude falling off with distance; gentle wind from the left. **The mask excludes the sheep body and its contact shadow** (dilated by 8 px with a soft edge), so the warp can never move the sheep | 12 s |
| Thin campfire smoke | noise wisp sampled on a 120 s circle, **drifting the same way as the grass sway** (left to right) | 120 s |
Natural motion only: the sheep breathes through its own flank. It never slides, rotates or scales as a whole.

## Music — LOCAL render (no GPU), ≈ 55 min set
**Sources:**
- Piano: **Salamander Grand Piano** (Yamaha C5, 16 velocity layers, CC-BY 3.0 by Alexander Holm, github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano). Credit in every description: "Piano samples: Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm (CC BY 3.0, creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)". It is sampled in minor thirds, so the renderer pitch-shifts by at most ±1 semitone. 16 layers keep the soft pp–mp playing alive; VCSL's Steinway B (2–3 layers) is not used for the piano.
- Strings: **VSCO-2 CE** (Versilian Studios, CC0): violin, viola and cello **section** sustains (vibrato, 2 dynamic layers, sampled every 2–4 semitones). Used only in the pad role under the piano, with pitch shift limited to ±2 semitones; no exposed solo cello lines (CE has no solo cello).
- Synth pads: our own local synth (detuned saw/sine stacks, slow attack, low-pass, chorus), no third-party patches.
- Renderer humanising: ±8 velocity, ±12 ms timing, sustain-pedal model with half-pedal, natural release tails.
Carols, all public domain, free arrangements (own reharmonisation, key and tempo; no copying of any recorded arrangement). The Redner tune is used for O Little Town.
