## What we change
1. **Detail level of plates** (biggest gap). Our F1 inn plate is clean and storybook-like; the winners are dense, layered and lit by many sources. G1 now compares every plate side by side at 1080p with The Forgotten Hearth's library and the Moria plate; ours must match their detail and depth, not just be "correct". Plate prompts get layered foreground/mid/background detail and many small light sources; plates go through a diffusion detail pass (not only Real-ESRGAN) before compositing.
2. **A story in the description**, second person, 4–6 short lines, like the Moria video (what you see, hear, why you are there).
3. **An opening hook** (first 30–60 s): candles/lanterns lighting up one by one, or dusk turning to night, before the loop starts.
4. **Thumbnail**: the richest crop of the plate, high warm/cool contrast; text optional (2–3 words) only if it wins the side-by-side.
5. **Honest positioning**: we use AI for pictures, so we cannot say "No AI". We win on care: original composed music and carols, natural motion, no stock loops, and a clear AI disclosure.

## What we do not copy
- **Lord of the Rings music or names.** Howard Shore's themes and the LOTR/Middle-earth names belong to their owners. Crediting the composer does not give permission; Content ID would claim or block the video, and the name is a trademark. We build our own worlds (the elven inn) and our own music.
- **Other people's music "in their own form"** (covers of modern pieces) needs a licence even when credited. Only public-domain melodies (composer died > 100 years ago, our own arrangement) or our own originals.
