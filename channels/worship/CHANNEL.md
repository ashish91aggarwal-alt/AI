## Promise
"A quiet place to pray." Long, gentle piano/strings/pad instrumentals for prayer,
devotion, Bible reading, rest and sleep. Reverent, warm, never dramatic.

## Audience
US-first Christian adults 25–65 (also UK, CA, AU, PH). They play it daily during
prayer and at night. Evidence (tracker, 2026-11-17): Perfect Harmony of Heaven (63K
subs) median like-rate 1.65%, one video at 103K views in a month; Calm Divine Flow
(23K) 1.4%; Holy Flame Catholic (33K) 4.4% with ~2 uploads/day; Central Record (141K).

Soft light scene (window light, candle, sunrise over water) + the caps phrase in a

## Sound palette
Felt/soft grand piano (Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm, CC BY 3.0,
creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/, 16 velocity layers, rendered locally; credit in every description), warm string pads and cello
(VSCO-2 CE, CC0), gentle synth pads (our own local synth), occasional soft choir "ahh" pad. Keys: D, E♭, A♭, F, B♭ major; 56–70 BPM.
Public-domain carols and hymns keep their printed mode (melody notes are never changed); their tonic
comes from the palette (e.g. O Come, O Come, Emmanuel in D minor/aeolian). Logged in docs/decisions.md.
No drums, no risers, no sudden dynamics. Loudness −20 to −18 LUFS.

## Melody sources
- Original "soaking" improvisations (majority of each video).
- Public-domain hymn melodies, composer died > 100 years ago: It Is Well (Bliss 1876),
  Abide With Me (Monk 1861), Be Thou My Vision (Slane, trad.), Amazing Grace (New Britain),
  What a Friend (Converse 1868), Holy Holy Holy (Dykes 1861), Nearer My God (Mason 1856),
  Come Thou Fount (Nettleton 1813), Christmas: Silent Night (Gruber 1818), O Holy Night
  (Adam 1847), O Come O Come Emmanuel (15th-c. chant, adapted by Thomas Helmore, 1856), Hark the Herald (Mendelssohn 1840),
  What Child Is This (Greensleeves, trad.), Away in a Manger (Kirkpatrick 1895).
- Avoid "Be Still My Soul / Finlandia" until 2028 (Sibelius d. 1957 — still protected in life+70 countries).
- **Content ID risk:** other channels' recordings of these hymns may be registered;
  a melody match can trigger a false claim on our own performance. Keep hymns to
  ≤ 30% of a video (≤ 50% for Christmas carol specials, measured in minutes), arrange freely (reharmonise, change tempo/key), dispute false claims
  with the arrangement files as proof.
