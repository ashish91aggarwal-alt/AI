## Scene — base plate
> Inside a tall elven library on a rainy afternoon, 16:9 wide view. Curved wooden
> bookshelves rising three levels with ladders, carved leaf motifs, a large round arched
> window with rain streaking down the glass and a misty green forest beyond, a reading
> desk in the foreground with stacked old books, an open book, a quill, and a brass
> oil lamp glowing warm. Soft grey daylight from the window mixing with warm lamp light,
> floating dust in the light, painterly realism, no people, no text. 4K.

**Separate element:** a tawny owl perched on a carved wooden reading stand, facing slightly
left, soft grey daylight from the right, plain neutral background.
