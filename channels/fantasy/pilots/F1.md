## Motion (G2) — loop length 90 s; every period divides 90 s
**Working rate for all I2V layers: 16 fps** (Wan 2.x native). All loop arithmetic below is in 16 fps frames. The finished 90 s loop is frame-interpolated to **30 fps** with an open model (RIFE, arbitrary timestep; 90 s × 30 = 2,700 frames) at G2 and checked again for seams.
**Every Wan I2V prompt includes "static locked-off camera, no camera movement".** Drift check on every clip: outside the motion mask (the fox's chest/flank, or the flames), the subject outline may move ≤ 1 px across the whole clip, measured by edge-map difference. A clip that fails is discarded, never stabilised by moving the picture.
| Layer | How | Period |
|---|---|---|
| Fox breathing (chest/flank only) | Wan 2.x I2V on the fox composite crop. **4 clips of 129 frames (8.06 s)** (the 129-frame length is tested automatically on the first breathing clip of session 2b; fallback 113 or 97 frames, see production/gpu/session2/PLAN.md). A sleeping fox breathes ≈ 20/min (3 s per breath); a native breath may be 2.61–3.53 s (the ±15% retime window), so every 8.06 s clip is guaranteed to hold at least one complete rest-to-rest breath plus its 0.25 s rest tail (8.06 − 3.53 − 0.25 = 4.28 s ≥ 3.53 s); 4 clips give ≥ 4 breaths, we need 3 from different clips. An automatic script on the pod (`check_clip.py`) finds the rest points and queues 1 more clip if fewer than 3 are usable. Cut **3 single breaths** from different clips at the end-of-exhale rest point, where the body is still. Each **segment = one breath retimed to 3.0 s + a 0.25 s rest tail = 3.25 s (52 frames)**. Join them in a ring A→B→C→A with **4-frame (0.25 s) crossfades** at the rest points: 3 × 3.25 − 3 × 0.25 = **9.0 s exactly** (144 frames), ×10 = 90 s. Seam test: frame difference at each join below the clip's own frame-to-frame noise | 9 s |
| Fox ear twitch | 2nd I2V clip (same prompt rules) of one ear flick, **played at real speed (no retime)**, inserted at 30 s and 75 s through an ear-only mask with a 0.5 s crossfade | 45 s |
| Fire | Wan I2V on the **painted hearth crop of our own plate** (so the fire keeps the painted style; no real footage to match). 3 different **5.5 s** clips (Wan gives 89 frames, trimmed to 88) A·B·C joined in a ring with 0.5 s (8-frame) crossfades: 3 × 5.5 − 3 × 0.5 = **15.0 s** pattern, ×6 = 90 s. **Only the flames are composited, through a soft feathered mask; the hearth stones, logs' surround and mantel stay the static plate.** The light-map flicker on the beams, rug and fox is driven by the luminance of that same fire layer, so it stays in sync and loops with it | 90 s |
| Embers | local particle sim, deterministic seeds, emitter pattern repeats every 90 s | 90 s |
| Snowfall in windows | 2 depth layers, masked to panes, particle schedule periodic at 90 s | 90 s |
| Lantern flicker | per-lantern light-map, out of phase, periods 9/15/18 s | ≤ 90 s |
| Mug steam | faint noise-displaced wisp, noise sampled on a 90 s circle | 90 s |
Natural motion only: the fox breathes and twitches through its own body. It never slides, rotates or scales as a whole. Loop seam check ×10 at G2.

**Required public-domain carols (3), ≤ 30% of runtime by measured minutes:**
- *In Dulci Jubilo* (14th-c. German melody): **G major**, 66 BPM
- *Coventry Carol* (16th-c. English): **A minor**, 58 BPM, with the printed major final chord kept
- *Wexford Carol* (traditional Irish): **G mixolydian**, 60 BPM. The printed flattened 7th is kept exactly as written (melody notes are never changed to fit a key). G is a palette tonic; the mode is a logged palette exception (CHANNEL.md: PD carols keep their printed mode on a palette tonic)
Tonics are all from the CHANNEL.md palette (D, A, G, E); In Dulci Jubilo and Coventry Carol are in palette modes too, Wexford is the one logged exception.
Each is a free arrangement written as MIDI by our code (own reharmonisation, tempo and key), then **rendered locally (no GPU) with CC0 samples from VCSL** (Versilian Community Sample Library: Baroque Alto/Tenor Recorder, Folk Harp, Concert Harp), using our own Python sample renderer. VCSL has 1 round robin and 2–3 velocity layers per note, so the renderer adds: natural release tails (the recorded release, or a measured exponential fade, never a hard cut), breath-shaped volume swells on held recorder notes, ±8 velocity and ±12 ms timing humanising, slight pitch drift (±3 cents) between repeated notes so repeats never sound identical, and no repeated identical sample start within 200 ms. **Room match:** the carols get a short convolution reverb, with the RT60 and EQ (spectral centroid, low-end balance) measured on the kept ACE-Step tracks so all three sit in the same room. They must match within ±15% RT60 and ±1.5 dB per octave band before the G3 listening gate, and are judged side by side with their neighbours in the running order. There is no lute (no CC0 lute exists that we could verify); the harp takes that role. ACE-Step is not used for the carols, so the melody stays exactly as written and no AI timbre transfer is involved. No copying of any recorded arrangement.

Running order: **59 min set = 8 originals × 5.5 min (44 min) + 3 carols ≈ 15 min (25% carols)**, inside G3's 45–60 min unique-music window. 3 h = **about 3 passes** (3 × 59 = 177 min + 3 min of pass 1 again), each pass in a different order; the carol share stays ≤ 30% in every pass and overall. If more than 8 originals pass G3, the extras are kept for F2, not added here.
Master: −17 LUFS integrated, ≤ −1 dBTP, 5 s crossfades.
